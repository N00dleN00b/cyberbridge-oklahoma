# Run of Show: Library / Community Family Night

**Length:** 60–75 minutes · **Audience:** students ages 11+ with parents, grandparents and caregivers · **Devices:** optional

Family nights reach the adults who are often the biggest scam targets, and the students teach alongside them.

| Time | Segment | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Arrival, snacks, sign-in (count only, no emails required) | Pre-survey optional for adults |
| 0:10 | Welcome + my story (from Oklahoma to China) | Module 3, step 1 |
| 0:17 | "Has this happened to you?" scam stories | Module 2, step 1; invite adults to share |
| 0:22 | **Phishing Spotter: families vs. kids** | Teams of kids vs. adults; it's fun when kids win |
| 0:35 | AI voice scams + family code word | Module 1, step 4; families pick their code word together |
| 0:42 | 2-step verification together | Help families turn it on for one account (they do it; you never touch passwords) |
| 0:52 | A view from China: digital life abroad | Global Perspectives, steps 1–2 |
| 1:00 | **Study abroad is for you: Gilman segment** | Emphasize affordability + FAFSA/Pell for parents |
| 1:05 | Q&A, handouts, post-survey | All handouts on a table |

## Partner ideas
Public libraries, Boys & Girls Clubs, 4-H, tribal community and education centers, churches, community action agencies, adult education / GED programs.
