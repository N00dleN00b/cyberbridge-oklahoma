# "Study Abroad Is for You" — Gilman Awareness Segment

**Use in every workshop.** 5 minutes. This is the part that fulfills the Gilman Follow-on Service Project goal: **increasing awareness of study abroad and the Gilman Scholarship** in your home community.

> Before presenting, check gilmanscholarship.org for current eligibility, deadlines and award amounts, and use the official [Gilman outreach materials](https://www.gilmanscholarship.org/resources/outreach-tools-resources/). If you show the Gilman logo, follow the Gilman Logo Usage Guidelines.

## Script 

**1. The surprise (30 sec)**
*"Quick question: how many of you think studying in another country is only for rich kids? … I used to think that too. I'm a first-generation college student from a low-income family, and I studied in China. Here's how."*

**2. What Gilman is (1 min)**
- The **Benjamin A. Gilman International Scholarship** is a U.S. Department of State program.
- It helps U.S. undergraduate students who receive a **Federal Pell Grant** study or intern abroad.
- It's designed to open study abroad to students who historically haven't had the chance, including first-generation students, students with financial need, and students in fields like STEM.
- Point students to **gilmanscholarship.org** for current details.

**3. My elevator speech (1.5 min)**
Use the Gilman Alumni Elevator Speech worksheet to prepare a 60–90 second version.

> *"I studied in China as a Gilman Scholar. I attended Shanghai University of Sport and China University of Petroleum in Beijing, learned about a range of topics, sat in on sample AI lectures, and stood on the Great Wall and walked through the Forbidden City. A few years ago I almost dropped out of college. What I brought home is proof that global opportunities are within reach for students like us, and a bigger picture of how technology and security work around the world. That's what this workshop is built on."*

**4. How to get there from here (1.5 min)**
- **Middle/high school students:** take school seriously, keep your grades up, and when you get to college (including community college), **find the study abroad office in your first year.** Apply for FAFSA every year; Pell Grant eligibility is the key to Gilman.
- **College students in the room:** visit your study abroad office this semester; look up Gilman deadlines; I'm happy to read your essay draft (see mentorship).
- **Parents/families:** study abroad can be affordable with scholarships. Encourage your student to ask.

**5. Call to action (30 sec)**
*"Scan this QR code for the Gilman website and my contact. If you want help with an application essay, reach out. Someone helped me; I'll help you."*
