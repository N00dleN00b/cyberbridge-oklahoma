# Run of Show: Educator & School Staff Workshop

**Length:** 60 minutes (fits a PD block or staff meeting) · **Audience:** teachers, librarians, school staff, administrators, nonprofit staff · **Devices:** one per participant helpful

Your proposal commits to workshops that demystify cybersecurity and AI for **students and educators**. Educators matter twice: they're frequent phishing targets for school districts, and they're the people who will keep teaching this curriculum after the project ends.

## Goals
Participants will:
1. Recognize the phishing and social engineering tactics most often aimed at school staff.
2. Understand basic security controls that protect student data (MFA, updates, backups, least privilege, incident reporting).
3. Use AI tools thoughtfully: what not to paste in, how to check output.
4. Feel ready to teach at least one CyberBridge module to their own students.
5. Know about study abroad and the Gilman Scholarship so they can encourage eligible students.

## Takeaway for staff: "Report it fast" card
- Clicked something suspicious? **Report it to your IT/tech coordinator immediately.** Speed limits damage; reporting is a control, not a confession.
- Never approve an MFA prompt you didn't start.
- Verify any request to change payroll, bank info or passwords by calling a known number.

## Note
Tie district-specific advice to the district's own policies and IT contacts. If you assess controls professionally for any of these schools, keep this workshop educational and separate from any formal assessment.
