# Pre/Post Survey (anonymous)

Give questions 1–6 before the workshop and all questions after. No names. Takes about 2 minutes. You can also put this in a Google Form or Microsoft Form (don't collect emails).

## Student survey

1. Grade: ___   Have you ever had a cybersecurity or computer science class? ☐ Yes ☐ No
2. **A text says you owe a $4 toll and must pay within 24 hours at a link. What do you do?**
   ☐ Pay it, it's only $4  ☐ Click to check  ☐ Ignore the link and check the official site/app myself  ☐ Not sure
3. **A friend asks you to send back a code they "accidentally" sent you. Should you?**  ☐ Yes ☐ No ☐ Not sure
4. **Which is a stronger password?** ☐ `Okla2010!` ☐ `Tractor-Pancake-Thunder-Okra` ☐ Not sure
5. I could see myself working in cybersecurity or technology someday.
   ☐ Strongly disagree ☐ Disagree ☐ Not sure ☐ Agree ☐ Strongly agree
6. Studying abroad (in another country) is something students like me can do.
   ☐ Strongly disagree ☐ Disagree ☐ Not sure ☐ Agree ☐ Strongly agree
