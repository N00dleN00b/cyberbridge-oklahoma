# Global Perspectives: China — Take-Home Card

**CyberBridge Oklahoma** · Global knowledge, local impact

## The journey
Bristie Rahman, a first-gen college student from Oklahoma, studied in China as a Gilman Scholar: **Shanghai University of Sport**, **China University of Petroleum (Beijing)**, sample **AI lectures**, the **Forbidden City** and the **Great Wall**.

## Digital life, two countries
| | China | United States |
|---|---|---|
| Paying | Phone QR-code payments are used for everyday purchases | Cards and tap-to-pay are most common |
| Apps | "Super apps" combine chat, payments, rides and more | Usually separate apps for each task |
| Data laws | National laws on cybersecurity, data security and personal information | No single national privacy law: federal rules for specific areas plus state laws |

**Big question:** How much convenience is worth how much data? There's no single right answer, but you should get to decide.

## Traveling? Protect your data
Update devices · turn on multi-factor authentication (make sure codes work abroad) · back up first · careful on public Wi-Fi · learn local rules before you go

## You can study abroad too
The **Benjamin A. Gilman International Scholarship** helps U.S. undergraduates who receive a Federal Pell Grant study or intern abroad. Learn more: **gilmanscholarship.org**
Ask your college's study abroad office about programs and deadlines.
