# Curriculum

Three core modules named in the Gilman Follow-on Service Project proposal, plus a global perspectives lesson. Each is standalone. Teach them in order for a full series, or pick one for a single visit.

| Module | Big idea | Students leave able to… |
|---|---|---|
| [1. AI in Everyday Life](module-1-ai-in-everyday-life/) | AI is already in your pocket, and it learns from data, including yours | Spot AI in apps they use; explain in one sentence how machine learning learns; question AI output instead of trusting it blindly |
| [2. Understanding Security Controls](module-2-understanding-security-controls/) | Layers of controls protect schools, hospitals and families, and people are one of the most important controls | Sort controls (preventive/detective/corrective; technical/administrative/physical); recognize phishing; use passphrases and MFA; know what to do after a mistake |
| [3. Pathways to Cybersecurity Careers](module-3-pathways-to-cyber-careers/) | There's a path into this field from where you are right now | Name 3 cyber roles; find a free next step (CyberPatriot, picoCTF, etc.); see someone like them in the field |
| [Global Perspectives: China](global-perspectives-china/) | Digital life and digital rules look different around the world | Compare how people in China and the U.S. pay, communicate and protect data; see study abroad as possible for them |

## What's in every module folder

- `lesson-plan.md` — objectives, timing, step-by-step script, unplugged option
- `slides-outline.md` — slide-by-slide content to paste into Google Slides, PowerPoint or Canva
- `activity.md` — the hands-on part
- `quiz.md` — 5 questions + answer key (use as exit ticket)
- `handout.md` — one-page take-home for students and families

## Standards alignment

Activities support the CSTA K–12 Computer Science Standards strands on **Network & the Internet (cybersecurity)** and **Impacts of Computing**, and are designed to fit Oklahoma Academic Standards for Computer Science in the same strands. Teachers should confirm specific standard codes against the current published standards for their grade band.
