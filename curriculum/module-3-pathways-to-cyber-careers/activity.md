# Activity: Career Match

**Time:** 15 minutes · **Groups of 3–4**

Print the role cards and the day-in-the-life cards separately. Groups match them, then each student picks the role they'd most like to try.

## Role cards → Day-in-the-life (answer key)

| Role | A day in the life |
|---|---|
| **Security Analyst (SOC Analyst)** | "I watch alerts from our network, figure out which ones are real attacks, and jump in to stop them." |
| **Penetration Tester (Ethical Hacker)** | "Companies hire me to try to break into their systems, with permission, so they can fix weaknesses before criminals find them." |
| **Security Control Assessor / GRC Analyst** | "I check whether an organization is following its security rules and laws, write up what's missing, and help them fix it." |
| **Digital Forensics Investigator** | "After an attack, I collect clues from computers and phones to figure out what happened, sometimes for court cases." |
| **Cloud Security Engineer** | "I set up and protect the systems that run apps and websites in the cloud." |
| **AI/ML Engineer** | "I train models on data and test whether they're accurate and fair before they're used." |
| **Security Awareness Trainer** | "I teach people how to spot scams and protect themselves. Basically what's happening in this room right now!" |
| **Incident Responder** | "When a hospital or school gets hit by ransomware, I'm on the team that contains it and gets things running again." |

## Discussion after matching
- Which job surprised you?
- Which ones need lots of coding? Which need more communication and writing?
- Which skills do you *already* have? (Problem solving, noticing details, explaining things, gaming persistence, helping family with tech.)

## Write it down
On a sticky note: *"The role I'd want to try is ____ because ____."* Collect for your impact notes (no names needed).
