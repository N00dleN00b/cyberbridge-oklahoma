# Pathways to Cybersecurity Careers — Take-Home Card

**CyberBridge Oklahoma** · You belong in this field.

## Roles to look up
Security Analyst · Ethical Hacker · Security Control Assessor · Digital Forensics · Cloud Security · AI/ML Engineer · Incident Responder · Security Awareness Trainer

## Free next steps (circle one to try this month)
- ☐ **CyberPatriot** — national youth cyber defense competition (ask a teacher, counselor, JROTC or club leader about starting a team)
- ☐ **picoCTF** — free security puzzles for beginners (picoctf.org)
- ☐ **Free online intro courses** — e.g. Cisco Networking Academy intro to cybersecurity
- ☐ **Oklahoma CareerTech** — technology center and community college cyber/IT programs
- ☐ **Talk to a mentor** — see contact below

## Scholarships to know
- **CyberCorps® Scholarship for Service** — college scholarships in exchange for government cyber service
- **Benjamin A. Gilman International Scholarship** — helps U.S. undergraduates who receive a Federal Pell Grant study or intern abroad. Learn more at gilmanscholarship.org

## Remember
You don't need to have it all figured out. Start curious. Keep going. **Resilience matters more than where you started.**
