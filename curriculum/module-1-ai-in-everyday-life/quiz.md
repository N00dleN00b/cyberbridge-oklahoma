# Module 1 Exit Ticket: AI in Everyday Life

1. Which of these uses AI?
   a) A calculator  b) A light switch  c) Your video app's recommended feed  d) A paper map

2. Machine learning works by…
   a) Following a list of rules a person wrote  b) Finding patterns in lots of examples  c) Reading your mind  d) Searching the internet for the answer

3. A chatbot gives you a fact for your history homework. What should you do?
   a) Copy it, chatbots are always right  b) Check it against a reliable source  c) Ask the chatbot if it's sure, and trust whatever it says  d) Ignore all AI forever

4. A face-recognition AI was trained mostly on photos of one group of people. What's a likely problem?
   a) It will be slower  b) It may be less accurate for people who weren't in the training data  c) It will refuse to work  d) No problem at all

5. True or false: Apps learn from what you click, watch and skip.

---

## Answer key
1. c  2. b  3. b  4. b  5. True
