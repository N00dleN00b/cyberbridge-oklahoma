# AI in Everyday Life — Take-Home Card

**CyberBridge Oklahoma** · Free lessons for Oklahoma students and families

## What is AI?
Computer programs that recognize, recommend, predict or create things. Most modern AI uses **machine learning**: it learns patterns from lots of examples.

## Where you already use it
Video and music recommendations · face unlock · photo filters · maps and traffic · spam filters · voice assistants · chatbots

## Remember
- **AI learns from examples, including you.** Your likes, skips and searches train your feed.
- **AI can be confidently wrong.** Check important answers with a trusted source.
- **AI can be unfair** when its training data leaves people out.
- **AI can fake voices and photos.** If someone calls in a panic asking for money, hang up and call them back on a number you know.

## Try this at home
Teach someone in your family one thing from today. Ask them: *"Can you name three apps on your phone that use AI?"*

## Family safety tip
Choose a **family code word** that only you know. If anyone calls claiming to be a family member in trouble, ask for the code word.
