# Understanding Security Controls — Take-Home Card

**CyberBridge Oklahoma** · Be a protector, not a bystander

## What's a security control?
Anything that protects information. Good security **stacks** them:
- **Preventive** (stop it): MFA, updates, training
- **Detective** (notice it): sign-in alerts, cameras
- **Corrective** (fix it): backups, a plan for what to do
And **you** are one of the most important controls.

## Spot a scam: 5 red flags
1. ⏰ **Rushes you** ("24 hours or your account is gone")
2. 🎁 **Too good to be true** (free money, prizes, easy high-paying jobs)
3. 🔗 **Links that don't match** or misspelled websites
4. 🔑 **Asks for passwords, login codes, gift cards or bank info**
5. 👤 **Strange sender**, or a friend acting unusual

**Not sure? Stop. Think. Check it another way.** Open the app or website yourself. Call the person on a number you already know.

## Lock down your accounts
- Use **long passphrases**: 4+ random words (`Tractor-Pancake-Thunder-Okra`)
- **Never reuse** passwords
- **Turn on multi-factor authentication (MFA)** for email, social media and games
- **Never share login codes** with anyone
- **Install updates** and **back up** photos and schoolwork

## Made a mistake?
Tell a trusted adult → change your password → turn on multi-factor authentication (MFA) → report the message. Fast action matters more than embarrassment.
