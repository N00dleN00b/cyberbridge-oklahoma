# Module 2 Exit Ticket: Understanding Security Controls

1. A backup of your files is mainly a ____ control.
   a) Preventive  b) Detective  c) Corrective  d) Physical

2. Which is the biggest red flag in a message?
   a) It uses your name  b) It says you must act within 24 hours or lose your account  c) It has a logo  d) It's in English

3. A "friend" texts asking you to send back a code they "accidentally" sent you. You should…
   a) Send it  b) Never send it; it can let someone take over your account  c) Send half of it  d) Post it publicly

4. Which password is strongest?
   a) `Password1!`  b) `Okla2010`  c) `Tractor-Pancake-Thunder-Okra`  d) Your pet's name

5. A school district wants to stop staff from falling for phishing. Which TWO controls help most?
   a) Training and multi-factor authentication  b) Bigger monitors and faster Wi-Fi  c) Security cameras and fire extinguishers  d) Nothing can help

6. You typed your password into a fake site. What's the FIRST thing to do?
   a) Hide it  b) Tell a trusted adult and change your password right away  c) Click it again  d) Delete your phone

---

## Answer key
1. c  2. b  3. b  4. c  5. a  6. b
