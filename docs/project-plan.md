# Project Plan: CyberBridge Oklahoma

Gilman Follow-on Service Project · Created by Bristie Rahman

## 1. Problem

Many rural and low-income Oklahoma high schools offer no cybersecurity or computer science courses. Students in these communities face the same digital threats as everyone else, including phishing, scams, account takeovers and data misuse, but are rarely taught how to recognize them, and rarely see cybersecurity, AI or study abroad as open to people like them.

## 2. Goals

1. **Gilman goal:** increase awareness of **study abroad and the Gilman Scholarship** among first-generation, low-income and rural students, and among college peers who could apply.
2. **Community goal (from the proposal):** demystify cybersecurity and AI for Oklahoma students and educators, connect global trends to local threats, and help students see themselves as future protectors and leaders.

## 4. Changes from the original proposal

- **Study location:** China instead of Korea (approved by the Gilman Program).
- **Delivery:** Bristie moved for a new career opportunity and no longer works at OCII, which limited time for in-person workshops around the state. To keep the project's reach, she built every workshop as a ready-to-run guide that teachers, librarians and outreach teams can deliver without her, shared the curriculum with OCII's AI Studio team, and is delivering a peer session and online outreach herself.

## 6. Audience

- **College peers**, especially first-generation, Pell-eligible and STEM students, who can apply for Gilman
- **Students, educators and families** in Oklahoma, reached through the free curriculum and the people who teach it
- **Professional network on LinkedIn**, including people in cybersecurity and education who can pass the materials on

## 7. Sustainability

Every lesson can be run from the lesson plan alone by someone with no cyber background, has an unplugged version, and is openly licensed. The materials stay on GitHub for OCII's outreach team, schools and libraries to use after the project ends.
