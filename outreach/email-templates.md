# Outreach Email Templates

For anyone running CyberBridge workshops. Replace the text in [brackets] with your own details.

## 1. To a school (principal, counselor, CS/tech teacher)

**Subject:** Free cybersecurity & AI workshop for your students (one class period)

Hi [Name],

I'd like to offer your students a free, interactive workshop from CyberBridge Oklahoma, an open curriculum created by Gilman Scholar Bristie Rahman. In one class period, students learn to:
- Spot phishing and scams aimed at them and their families
- Understand the basic security controls that protect their school's data
- See how AI shows up in their daily lives, and how it can get things wrong
- Explore cybersecurity career pathways and scholarships, including study abroad through the Gilman Scholarship

It needs no prep from your teachers and works with or without devices. I can also run a short session for staff on phishing and protecting student data.

Would a date in [month] work?

Thank you,
[Your name] · [Organization] · [Contact]
