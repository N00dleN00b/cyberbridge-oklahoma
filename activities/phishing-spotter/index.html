Remember: SLAM

  • Sender: is it really who it claims to be?
  • Links: does the real address match? Is it misspelled?
  • Attachments: were you expecting it?
  • Message: is it rushing you, scaring you, or asking for codes, passwords or money?

Not sure? Stop. Think. Check it another way. Open the app or website yourself, or ask a trusted adult.
